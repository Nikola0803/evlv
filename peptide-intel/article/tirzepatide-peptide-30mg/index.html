Reconstitution Protocol for Research Applications

The following reconstitution approach is standard for tirzepatide peptide in laboratory settings. All work should be performed under appropriate sterile conditions for the intended application:

Standard laboratory reconstitution

  1. Allow the lyophilized vial to equilibrate to room temperature (15–30 minutes) before opening to prevent condensation from compromising the powder
  2. Prepare the required volume of bacteriostatic water or sterile water for injection (for research purposes only)
  3. Add diluent slowly down the side of the vial — never directly onto the lyophilized cake — using a sterile syringe
  4. Swirl gently to dissolve. Do not vortex or shake — this can cause peptide aggregation and reduce effective concentration
  5. Allow 2–5 minutes for complete dissolution; tirzepatide at 30mg in 3ml achieves a 10mg/ml concentration
  6. Inspect visually for clarity — a properly reconstituted solution should be clear and colourless

Working concentration preparation

For most cell-based assays, working stocks are prepared by serial dilution from the reconstituted stock into appropriate assay buffer. Typical research working concentrations range from 0.1 nM to 100 nM depending on the receptor system and assay format. Document each dilution step with date, target concentration, and diluent used.

Storage of reconstituted tirzepatide

  • Short-term (up to 7 days): 4°C in sealed vial; minimise freeze-thaw cycles
  • Long-term: Aliquot into single-use volumes and store at -20°C; avoid repeated freeze-thaw
  • Never store at room temperature after reconstitution

Purity Verification for Tirzepatide Peptide 30mg

Given tirzepatide's structural complexity (39 amino acids, fatty acid modification), purity verification is more demanding than for smaller peptides. Researchers should require:

  • HPLC purity ≥98% — the chromatogram should show a single dominant peak with minimal baseline noise. For tirzepatide specifically, look for absence of deletion sequence peaks in the 4200–4600 Da range
  • High-resolution mass spectrometry — confirming the [M+6H]⁶⁺ and [M+7H]⁷⁺ charge states for tirzepatide (expected m/z approximately 802.9 and 688.1 respectively)
  • Peptide content assay — some suppliers report "purity" based on HPLC area percentage, which includes water and counter-ions; a peptide content assay (amino acid analysis) gives true compound mass
